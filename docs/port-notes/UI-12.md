# Port note: UI-12 (the layout grid)

Package UI-12 of the UI refresh ([docs/specs/ui-refresh-plan.md](../specs/ui-refresh-plan.md), section 7's UI-12 row). The owner's decision of 2026-11-21
replaces "restyle only, layouts unchanged": **every window is laid out again on one spacing grid by a tool** (same controls, same window sizes where they
fit), a pilot first (this package, branch `ui/ui12p-1`), then every window in slices; and **the check box's box is on the left of its text,
everywhere**. Why: the owner's review of the UI-10 text-fit galleries ("overall not good. UI lacking consistence. choice of spacing not symetrical"; the
38 rows of [UI-12-owner-review.tsv](UI-12-owner-review.tsv)). The owner's standard for the result: "apply general styling sense. Looking for symmetry,
consistency, right balance between too much crowding and too much spacing". The numbers below are the starting point, not the goal.

## 1. The grid

In pixels at 96 dpi (100%), for the program's font after UI-2b (Segoe UI 9 pt). The planner reads every number from one table at the top of
`tools/UiFit/grid_tools.py` (`GRID`), so a change here is a change there.

- **Container padding** 8 px on every side (form client area, panel, tab page, group box). A group box's content starts 8 px below its title band;
  nothing covers a title.
- **Labels.** A label sits 6 px before its field, text right-aligned, vertically centred on the field. Labels of one column share their right edge;
  fields of one column share their left edge.
- **Single-line fields** (text box, combo box, date picker, number field: `ValidDouble`, `ValidNum`, up-down boxes and the like) use one standard
  height from the font. Rows have one pitch per container: the standard height + 6 px gap (no two fields touch). Consecutive check boxes or radio
  buttons form a list with a compact pitch (box height + 4). A button in a field row has the field height.
- **Widths.** Fields of one column whose right edges are within about 16 px of each other share one right edge; other widths snap to the 4 px grid.
  Multi-line boxes, grids, lists and pictures keep their size, snapped to the grid, with their edges aligned with the column they belong to.
- **Check boxes.** `CheckAlign` MiddleLeft, `TextAlign` MiddleLeft, the box first. A check box in a column of label + field rows puts its box at the
  field column's left edge (its text after it); a check box in a check box list or a group of check boxes only puts its box at the content's left
  edge. AutoSize where the text fits in the row; otherwise the text wraps into a taller row. A control beside it (the "Sync" button) follows the text
  with 6 px.
- **Containers side by side** have 8 px between them. **Dialog buttons** (OK, Save, Cancel, Close) sit bottom right, 8 px apart, 8 px from the edges.
- **Growth.** A container grows (down first, then right) when its content needs it; the window grows only when it must, and never shrinks; the
  Preferences pages keep the width FormPreferences gives them and grow down.
- **Untouched:** Dock and Anchor values, tab order, names, events, texts, docked controls, controls the code positions at run time (`Location`,
  `Bounds`, `Left`, `Top`, `Size` set in the window's `.cs`, or `LayoutManager.Move*`), controls stacked on the same bounds and switched by `Visible`
  at run time (they move together as one), and owner-drawn surfaces (the tooth chart, the appointment book, the sheet and eForm fill surfaces, claim
  forms, printouts).
