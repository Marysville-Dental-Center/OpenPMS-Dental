# Port note: UI-12 (the layout grid)

Package UI-12 of the UI refresh ([docs/specs/ui-refresh-plan.md](../specs/ui-refresh-plan.md), section 7's UI-12 row). The owner's decision of 2026-11-21
replaces "restyle only, layouts unchanged": **every window is laid out again on one spacing grid by a tool** (same controls, same window sizes where they
fit), a pilot first (this package, branch `ui/ui12p-1`), then every window in slices; and **the check box's box is on the left of its text,
everywhere**. Why: the owner's review of the UI-10 text-fit galleries ("overall not good. UI lacking consistence. choice of spacing not symetrical"; the
38 rows of [UI-12-owner-review.tsv](UI-12-owner-review.tsv)). The owner's standard for the result: "apply general styling sense. Looking for symmetry,
consistency, right balance between too much crowding and too much spacing". The numbers below are the starting point, not the goal.

## 1. The grid

In pixels at 96 dpi (100%), for the program's font after UI-2b (Segoe UI 9 pt). The planner reads every number from one table at the top of
`tools/UiFit/grid_tools.py` (`GRID`), so a change here is a change there.

- **Container padding** 8 px on every side (form client area, panel, tab page, group box). A group box's content starts 8 px below its title band;
  nothing covers a title.
- **Labels.** A label sits 6 px before its field, text right-aligned, vertically centred on the field. Labels of one column share their right edge;
  fields of one column share their left edge.
- **Single-line fields** (text box, combo box, date picker, number field: `ValidDouble`, `ValidNum`, up-down boxes and the like) use one standard
  height from the font. Rows have one pitch per container: the standard height + 4 px gap (the owner, round 2: fields 23 px, row pitch 27;
  round 1 had 6 px). Consecutive check boxes or radio buttons form a list with a compact pitch (box height + 4); rows that both start with a
  check box in one column are one list even when a field or note stands further along a row. A button in a field row has the field height.
- **Preferences style** (round 2, the lead's audit; every Preferences page and any group of mostly check boxes): a label starts where the
  check box text beside it starts (the box's x + 16 px), left-aligned; every field of the page shares **one left edge**, the same in every
  group (the widest label + 6 px, measured over the page in page coordinates); a label that would push that column past **50%** of the
  content width wraps onto two lines instead (the lead's decision of round 3, "not 60%": on the 494 px page the field column is at 256,
  rounds 2 and 3 had 304). Combo boxes, note boxes and fields 120 px or wider end at the content's right edge (one right
  edge for the page); narrow number boxes keep their width. A group box alone on its rows spans the page.
- **Shared field columns** (round 2): in ordinary forms the field columns of different containers (group boxes, tab pages, panels, the
  window) whose left edges are within 40 px in window coordinates share the rightmost one, so the fields of one column of the window start
  at one x across the group boxes stacked in it. Group boxes stacked in one column share one right edge. A check box in a group of label +
  field rows puts its box on the group's main field column.
- **Widths.** Fields of one column whose right edges are within about 16 px of each other share one right edge; other widths snap to the 4 px grid.
  Multi-line boxes, grids, lists and pictures keep their size, snapped to the grid, with their edges aligned with the column they belong to.
- **Check boxes.** `CheckAlign` MiddleLeft, `TextAlign` MiddleLeft, the box first. A check box in a column of label + field rows puts its box at the
  field column's left edge (its text after it); a check box in a check box list or a group of check boxes only puts its box at the content's left
  edge. AutoSize where the text fits in the row; otherwise the text wraps into a taller row. A control beside it (the "Sync" button) follows the text
  with 6 px.
- **Containers side by side** have 8 px between them. **Dialog buttons** (OK, Save, Cancel, Close) sit bottom right, 8 px apart, 8 px from the edges.
- **Growth.** A container grows (down first, then right) when its content needs it; the window grows only when it must, and never shrinks; the
  Preferences pages keep the width FormPreferences gives them and grow down.
- **Round 3** (the lead's audit of round 2 and the owner's addition "Drop down, radio, text also. Spacing between items should be the same.
  Text shouldn't be covered by other elements"): rows of one column that end in a field end at one right edge (the column's most common
  edge or its widest, within 16 px: City, ST, Zip under Address); lists side by side in a row get one top and one height, the captions over
  them start where they start, a row of buttons under them ends where the last list ends; the parts beside the lists of a column start at
  one x and the rightmost box of each row of a column ends at one edge (FormPractice's right column); a check box hidden in the designer on
  a row of shown controls and taller than they are goes to the end of its column; a one-line check box within 3 px of its row stays one
  line (GDI+ measures 2-3 px wider than the box draws); a tall list keeps the rows beside it in its row; the stock radio button is drawn
  like the check box (`UiRadioLook`: the circle where the box is, the text 16 px from the left), so radio buttons and check boxes share x
  and pitch.
- **Round 4** (the fix round, the lead's review of round 3): a **clinic picker** is a single-line field like every combo box: 23 px tall
  (the planner left it at the designer's 21), its own "Clinic" label drawn right-aligned 6 px before its box and centred on it, like the
  labels of the rows around it (it was drawn from x = 2 at a fixed top, touching the box); a **multi-line text box one line tall** (Referred
  From, FormScreenEdit's Birthdate) is a single-line field: 23 px, the row pitch and the shared single-line frame (`UiInputFrame`); a
  **wrapped note** is as tall as its lines and no taller (the larger of two estimates: a word wrap at the measured average character width,
  and the measured width over the room), so a group's bottom padding under a note is the 8 px of every group; a **row hidden in the
  designer** at the end of a group either stands beside a shown row where it fits (UserControlEnterpriseGeneral's lock note, never shown on
  that page) or the group ends 8 px under its last shown row and the code that shows the row grows the group by it and moves what is under
  it (FormPatientEdit's super family row, in Load; UserControlMainWindow's restricted clinics row, from FormPreferences after FillSynced);
  scaled (150%), the **program's combo box and the clinic picker** take the height of a single-line text box at the font text boxes get
  (LayoutManager), because the Windows inputs (text boxes, number fields, date pickers) take their height from their font and cannot be
  sized: one row of inputs has one height at every scale. **Code that sizes the window itself** (`Width`, `Height`, `Size`, `ClientSize`
  set on the form) is listed by the planner for every window (tool and hand), so a slice checks each size against the new layout; the
  pilot's two that broke are now derived from the designer (FormInsBenefits' simplified view width, FormPayment's hidden-splits height).
- **Round 5** (the second fix round, the lead's review of round 4): a **wider gap** between two rows (the section gap, 12 px) stays only
  where something explains it: a row hidden in the designer stood in it, or one of the two rows is a visible separator line (a panel or
  label 1-3 px tall); otherwise the rows take the gap of their kind (UserControlMainWindowMisc kept main's loose 12 px between two groups
  and between two fields). A **label that wraps beside its field** (the Preferences style) is as tall as its measured lines: a word wrap
  with the advance of each character as a label draws it (Segoe UI 9, measured with GDI+), scaled to the dump's measured one-line width;
  a line end after the text draws nothing (the width estimate made a two-line label three lines tall, 12 px off its field's centre, and
  missed the third line of "DateTime the currently running aging started / Usually blank"). **No line starts with a hyphen**: when the
  wrap would break a hyphenated word before its hyphen ("non" / "-primary"), the label narrows to halfway between the line without the
  word and the line with its first part, so the whole word moves down.
- **Untouched:** Dock and Anchor values, tab order, names, events, texts, docked controls, controls the code positions at run time (`Location`,
  `Bounds`, `Left`, `Top`, `Size` set in the window's `.cs`, or `LayoutManager.Move*`), controls stacked on the same bounds and switched by `Visible`
  at run time (they move together as one), and owner-drawn surfaces (the tooth chart, the appointment book, the sheet and eForm fill surfaces, claim
  forms, printouts).

From the control tree of the 47 pilot windows at Segoe UI 9 (`GridDump`, section 2):

| Number | Value | Measured / why |
|---|---|---|
| Single-line field height | **23 px** | `TextBox.PreferredHeight` of a bordered single-line text box at the program's font is 23 in every pilot window. The date picker and the up-down box take 23 from the font and cannot be made smaller, so 23 is the one height every input can share (the owner's FormPayment row asks for one look). The planner reads it from each window's tree (bordered text boxes only: a borderless box is 16). |
| Row pitch | **27 px** (23 + 4) | The owner's answer of 2026-11-21 (round 2); round 1 used 29 (23 + 6), which made long forms pass 1000 px. |
| Check box / radio row | **18 px**, list pitch **22 px** | `UI.CheckBox`'s own height; the text line measured by GDI+ is 16-17 px, so max(18, line + 2). |
| Group box title band | **18 px** | 1 px offset + the title's GDI+ line height (17). |
| Content below the title | **4 px** (not 8) | Refined from the first gallery pass: the band's bottom already holds the font's internal leading, so 8 px below it put the first row 12 px under the title's letters, against 8 px at the box's bottom: the top looked loose and the box unbalanced. 4 px gives the same 8 px of air under the letters as at the bottom. |
| Section gap | **12 px** | Rows that were 12 px or more apart keep 12 px when a row hidden in the designer stood between them or one of them is a separator line (round 5; before, every such gap stayed, main's loose spacing too). It is not above the gap the grid makes, so a second run keeps it. |
| Caption gap | **2 px** | A label standing above the control it names (a list's title). |
| Check box lead | **16 px** | `UI.CheckBox`: 12 px box + 1 px line + 3 px before the text; the stock check box 18 px. |
| Band gap | **16 px** | Between two label + field bands of one row (where a window has two columns of fields). |

## 2. The tool, and how a rollout slice runs it

**The parts.**
- `tests/UiRefreshTests/GridDump.cs` (opt-in, `OPMS_UI_GRID_DUMP` names the output TSV, `OPMS_UI_GRID_WINDOWS` the windows; default the windows
  of `Data/grid-windows.tsv`): builds each window off screen at the program's font (the harness of the UI-10 text-fit scan, `WindowBuilder`), and
  writes every control: bounds, client area, dock, anchor, AutoSize, CheckAlign, TextAlign, the text on one line measured the way the control
  draws it, a group box's title band, a text box's own height.
- `tools/UiFit/grid_tools.py plan --tree <dump> --out <plan.tsv> --unplaced <tsv>` (the planner): reads the tree with each window's designer and
  code files (`fit_tools.window_files`; controls the code positions are found in the window's `.cs` files: `Location`, `Left`, `Top`, `Bounds`,
  `Size`, `Width`, `Height` set, `SetBounds`, `LayoutManager.Move*`). Per container, children first: it merges controls stacked on the same
  bounds (switched by `Visible` at run time) into one item, cuts the container into rows (and, where no row line exists, into columns; a label
  is never parted from the field it names), pairs each label with its field, clusters the fields of a column by their left edge, and places
  everything by the rules of section 1 (the numbers are the table `GRID` at the top of the file). Containers grow to their content; a group box
  also shrinks to it (equal padding top and bottom); containers that nearly span their parent span it; group boxes side by side, and the i-th
  boxes of columns of boxes, get one top and one height; the window's bottom bar sits 8 px from the bottom with OK/Save/Cancel/Close bottom right.
  **The rules run again on their own result until a pass changes nothing** (at most 6 passes): one pass is not a fixed point, because the cut
  of the laid-out window can find other rows than the cut of the drawn one. The plan is the last pass against the drawn window, so **a plan
  made from the build of an applied plan is empty** (the pilot's re-run: section 4). The plan TSV: designer file, window, control, property
  (Location, Size, ClientSize, AutoSize, CheckAlign, TextAlign), old, new, rule. The `--unplaced` TSV lists what the planner could not place
  (overlapping controls kept as drawn, check boxes with no room to wrap, the windows by hand) and its own check of the planned layout.
  A stale tree (the designer files no longer match the build) is refused per window.
- `grid_tools.py apply --plan <plan.tsv>` (the applier): edits the designer files as bytes (latin-1 round trip), changing only the digits of a
  `Location`/`Size`/`ClientSize` line or one alignment word, or inserting one line before the control's `Name` line with that line's own line
  end: every line end and BOM stay (`tools/Test-NoEolFlips.ps1 -Base main`: 0 flips). The same applier writes the hand plan
  `tests/UiRefreshTests/Data/grid-hand.tsv` (the same columns), so hand edits are data too and re-apply to the same bytes.
- `tests/UiRefreshTests/Data/grid-windows.tsv`: window, slice, mode (`tool`: the planner lays it out; `hand`: listed, its edits are hand rows), note.
- `tests/UiRefreshTests/Data/grid-scope.tsv` (round 2): window, container, controls, keep, note. A window listed here is laid out **in
  parts**: each row is a container laid out whole (its path in the tree) or named controls of a container laid out as one region (`keep`
  = `width`: the part keeps its width and its fields shrink to the room). When a part grows, what stands below it keeps its distance to it
  (moves down), what stands right of it moves right, its container and the window grow by as much, and controls on the window's bottom
  edge stay on it (tall ones grow). Every distance is measured on the window as it was before the pass, so two parts side by side push
  once. This is the "keep the width" mode the rollout needs for windows whose other parts the code lays out (FormApptEdit, FormPayment).
- Round 2 planner rules besides section 1: date pickers (`ODDatePicker`, its box 63 px inside its bounds) and clinic pickers (their own
  label in the first 37 px) are aligned by their box; a `RightToLeft` check box or radio button counts as box-right (the applier sets
  `RightToLeft.No`); columns that hold group boxes are cut as columns before rows (no band of empty space beside a tall box); a row of
  controls hidden in the designer goes to the end of its column; a check box drawn taller than its one line of text is one line tall;
  fields narrowed in one pass widen again when their container grew; notes and their buttons one per row are centred on their column; a
  wide note box keeps its right edge when its column moves right; a wide grid ending a little short of its column reaches it; the bottom
  bar's buttons stand on one line; a control only sized in code (`MoveHeight`, `Width =`) moves with its row; commented-out code is not
  read as positioning; a button that names the box beside it (Lab, Req) stands where a label would.
- `grid_tools.py plan --simulate --tree <a --tree-out file>`: plans the planned trees again without designer checks; the offline idempotence check.
- **Layout scripts** (round 3, `tools/UiFit/grid_hand.py` and one script per window in `tools/UiFit/hand/<Window>.py`): for windows the planner
  does not settle (nested panels, modules laid out by their code or by the Chart layout sheet), the script names the window's rows and columns
  and places them with the numbers of `GRID` (the field height measured from the tree, the pitch, the label gap, the padding, the title band):
  `Box.field(label, field, x, w, after=...)`, `Box.checks(...)`, `Box.place(...)`, `h.set(...)`. `grid_hand.py --tree <planned tree> --window
  <Window>` writes the rows into `grid-hand.tsv` (rule `by hand (layout script): ...`, replacing the window's earlier script rows) and prints
  its own check (overlaps by text, controls past their container, labels narrower than their text); `--dry --tree-out <tsv>` draws the result
  with `render.py` before a build. A window can mix the two: its tabs laid out by the planner in keep-width parts (`grid-scope.tsv`; the group
  boxes inside a keep-width part grow to their content, round 3) and the rest by its script, which reads the planner's tree.

**A rollout slice (UI-12-N).**
1. `git switch -c ui/ui12-N main`; add the slice's windows (the next files of `fit-scan-files.tsv` in rank order, about 40 designer files) to
   `grid-windows.tsv` with mode `tool`.
2. Build the filter through the lane (x64), then dump the tree of the slice's windows:
   `set OPMS_UI_GRID_DUMP=<folder>\tree-before.tsv` (and `OPMS_UI_GRID_WINDOWS=<types>` to dump only the slice), Targeted run of `UiRefreshTests`.
3. `python tools/UiFit/grid_tools.py plan --tree <folder>\tree-before.tsv --windows <types> --out <folder>\plan.tsv --unplaced <folder>\unplaced.tsv`,
   then `apply --plan <folder>\plan.tsv`. Commit.
4. Build `OpenPmsDental` and `UiRefreshTests` together (the test bin keeps its own copy of the dll), draw the gallery (`FitGallery` with
   `OPMS_UI_FIT_GALLERY_FONT=program`), and LOOK at every picture. A window the rules place badly: either a rule change in `GRID`/the planner
   (re-plan from main: restore the slice's designer files from `main`, plan from the tree of `main`, apply) or mode `hand` with hand rows.
5. Dump the after build and plan again: the plan must be empty (or only rows of controls of other designers, listed). Add the findings
   `GridRulesTests` reports that the planner also lists, with their reason, to `grid-known.tsv`; fix the rest.
6. Mark the slice's `fit-fixes.tsv` rows that the grid changed (`; superseded by the UI-12 grid`), rewrite the layout snapshot
   (`OPMS_UI_LAYOUT_SNAPSHOT_WRITE=1`), audit the gallery (`UI-12-audit.md` style), Targeted tests.

**Rules folded into the tool after looking at the pictures** (each repeated over several windows):
- A label is never parted from the field it names (a vertical cut between a label column and a field column made each its own stack and
